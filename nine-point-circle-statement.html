The Nine-Point Circle (1821)

Charles-Julien Brianchon and Jean-Victor Poncelet, 1821 · Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach, 1822

In any triangle, these nine points lie on one circle:

  • the middle points of the sides;
  • the feet of the heights;
  • the middle points of the lines from the corners to the point where the heights meet.
